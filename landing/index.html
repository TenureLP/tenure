How it works

Three acts, one transaction each.

No debt is created at any point. There is a sale, a lease, and a promise to sell back at a price fixed on day one.

Listedyou set the terms
Activefunded, leased back to you
Bought backyours again

01 · SELL

A financier buys your position.

You list your position NFT with a sale price, a rent and a buyback price. A financier pays the price in USDG and becomes the owner. Ownership really transfers: they can sell it on, and they carry its risk.

02 · LEASE

You keep working it.

It is leased straight back to you, for 1 to 30 days. You collect the swap fees it earns as often as you like. The rent is a fixed amount, prepaid, streamed to the owner by the second. Nobody can pull the liquidity.

03 · BUY BACK, OR DON'T

Your call at the end.

Pay the buyback price any time before the grace window closes and the position is yours again, with unused rent refunded. If you pass, the financier keeps what they already own. You owe nothing.

Two sides

Who it is for.

Liquidity providers

  • Cash today without unwinding the position or leaving the pool.
  • You keep the fee income for the whole lease.
  • One known cost: a fixed rent you set when listing.
  • No margin calls, no forced sale, no debt if you walk away.
Selling a position →

Financiers

  • You own a real, productive on-chain asset, not an IOU.
  • Fixed rent, prepaid into escrow and streamed per second.
  • Your side can be sold at any time during the lease.
  • Rent pauses while an issuer freezes a token in the pair, up to a ceiling you see before funding.

You carry the asset risk. If the position loses value and the seller does not buy back, that loss is yours. That risk is what the rent pays for.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this a loan under another name?

No. There is a sale, then a lease, then a one-sided promise by the new owner to sell back at a price fixed in advance, and never above the sale price. No debt exists at any point: if you never buy back, you owe nothing and nothing is seized, because the position already belongs to the financier.

Which positions are eligible?

Any Uniswap v4 position on Robinhood Chain that holds liquidity, is in range when listed and funded, and has no subscriber attached. The contract does not judge pools: whoever funds a deal decides whether it is a pool they want to own a piece of.

What does the protocol charge?

Nothing. The contract has no fee and no address it pays. A front end can ask to be paid through a builder code, and only the party that names it pays it, out of their own money, capped at 1% of the price.

What if a token in the pair gets frozen?

Rent stops accruing while the freeze is recorded, and the rent for that time goes back to the lessee at settlement, up to a ceiling of at most half the term that the financier sees before funding.

Who controls the contract?

Nobody. It has no owner, no admin, no pause and no upgrade path. Every bound on a deal is a constant in the code.

Is it audited?

Not yet. Do not put funds you cannot afford to lose into unaudited contracts, ours included. The audit report will be public before any mainnet deployment.

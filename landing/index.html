How it works

Three acts, one transaction each.

No debt is created at any point. There is a sale, a lease, and an option for you to buy back.

01 · SELL

A financier buys your position.

You list your position NFT with a sale price, a rent and a buyback price. A financier pays the price in USDG. Ownership really transfers: they can resell it, and they carry its risk.

02 · LEASE

You keep working it.

The position is leased back to you for 7 or 21 days. You collect the swap fees it earns, as often as you like. The rent is a fixed amount that streams to the owner by the second. Nobody can pull the liquidity.

03 · BUY BACK, OR DON'T

Your call at the end.

Pay the buyback price any time before the grace window closes and the position is yours again, with unused rent refunded. If you pass, the financier keeps what they already own. You owe nothing.

Two sides

Who it is for.

Liquidity providers

  • Cash today without unwinding the position or leaving the pool.
  • You keep the fee income for the whole lease.
  • One known cost: a rent in USDG that you set when listing.
  • No margin calls, no forced sale, no debt if you walk away.

Financiers

  • You own a real, productive on-chain asset, not an IOU.
  • Fixed rent, prepaid into escrow and streamed per second.
  • Your ownership is transferable at any time during the lease.
  • Rent pauses while an issuer freezes the underlying token.

You carry the asset risk. If the position loses value and the seller does not buy back, that loss is yours. That risk is what the rent pays for.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this a loan under another name?

No. There is a sale, then a lease, then a one-sided promise by the new owner to sell back at a price fixed in advance. No debt exists at any point: if you never buy back, you owe nothing and nothing is seized, because the position already belongs to the financier.

Which positions are eligible?

At launch, Uniswap v4 positions on Robinhood Chain in allowlisted pools, in range when listed, with no subscriber attached. Pools are reviewed one by one, and the reference of each review is recorded on-chain.

What about tokenized stocks?

The Stock Tokens live on Robinhood Chain today are debt securities that track a share price. They are not shares. Tenure will only allowlist tokenized equities that carry full shareholder rights, as they become available.

What does the protocol charge?

A flat fee per deal, in USDG, capped in the contract and fixed for your deal at the moment you list. Never a percentage of the amount.

What if the underlying token gets frozen?

Rent stops accruing for as long as the freeze lasts, and the rent for that time goes back to the lessee at settlement. The owner of an asset carries the risk that it becomes unusable.

Is it audited?

Not yet. Do not put funds you cannot afford to lose into unaudited contracts, ours included. The audit report will be public before any mainnet deployment.

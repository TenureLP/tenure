THE TOKEN

Nine things this token cannot do to you.

None of them exist in the contract, because it has no owner to call them. A test loops over all nine and fails if any of them ever resolves.
✗owner()
✗mint(address,uint256)
✗pause()
✗unpause()
✗blacklist(address)
✗setFee(uint256)
✗upgradeTo(address)
✗transferOwnership(address)
✗renounceOwnership()
It also entitles you to nothing at all today. No revenue reaches it, and the protocol takes no fee to give it any. That is written down, not implied.
